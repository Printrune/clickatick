# Homepage interaction and illustration audit v1

Date: 2026-10-18. Role: Frontend reviewer. Brief: `brief/project.json`, version 27.

Scope: front page and its expanded creation experience. Read-only source review. No source changes, deployment, paid AI requests, microphone use, camera use, or browser interaction. PM owns rendered verification. Findings below distinguish definite implementation limits from visual risks that need browser confirmation.

Approved intent: a neutral person with an idea bubble; the same bubble grows to contain voice or text conversation and an evolving event world. Confirmed answers change the illustration automatically. The Halloween example needs a guide, 20 guests, and chosen ticket format. Fast, smooth, futuristic, with no appearance menus. Existing approved direction remains the baseline.

## Principal finding

The implemented feature changes a few illustration properties, but it does not sustain the promised transformation. It consists of three separately rendered layouts, a repeated character image, and five scenery templates. The essential interaction and visual story are underdeveloped even if every control works.

## Findings and remedies

## Visual direction gaps to combine with Designer review

- The person has soft raster shading, while environment and ticket props use flat SVG shapes. The lack of shared material, lighting, edge, and perspective treatment needs rendered evaluation.
- The hero's stars, rings, glow, gradient type, small uppercase labels, and decorative `01` create atmosphere. They provide little explanation of event creation.
- Multiple nested rounded surfaces compete: outer bubble, scene frame, conversation panel, message wrapper, message paragraph, composer, preview panel, listing card.
- The initial headline does not identify ticketing or AI conversation. The supporting paragraph also avoids the concrete output “listing.” The header and footer carry more product explanation.
- The expanded world is a large rounded rectangle without the thought-bubble tail. The original visual metaphor becomes weaker after entry.

These are design diagnoses, not objective browser failures. PM and Designer should consolidate duplicates with the rendered audit.

## Priority sequence

1. Fix spatial continuity of bubble, character, scene, and active conversation.
2. Replace the repeated figure and scenery-template limitations with an authored event illustration system.
3. Make confirmed-answer changes visible, readable, and smoothly connected to the conversation.
4. Repair mobile composition, input states, contrast, focus, and recovery.
5. Refine type, icon consistency, decorative density, asset delivery, and small motion details.
