# Product marketing context

Version: 77
Business: clickatick
Buying context: b2b
Customer: Independent local event organisers and activity providers in the United States.
Offer: Quick ticket setup through a conversation, with event details and ticket types prepared for review.
Pricing model: percentage-of-ticket-price
Promise: Create tickets. Skip the hassle.
Positioning: clickatick helps local event and activity organizers create ticket listings quickly through voice or chat.
Difference: Proposed benefit: direct voice access and simple setup. Demand and differentiation remain unvalidated.
Journey: listing
Action: Create tickets
Scope: Start a listing draft without an account.; Have a natural conversation through live artificial intelligence using voice or text; show and speak agent replies.; Accept several details in one answer, remember supplied details, and ask focused follow-up questions only when needed.; Support single-date events, activity dates, weekly schedules, and multiple ticket types with separate prices and quantities.; Review and edit the listing draft; require login and an explicit Publish action before publication. Payments are outside the demonstration.

## Open questions
- Brand name availability remains unchecked.
- Before public activation: verify provider/model maximum costs, anonymous abuse controls, database permissions, email delivery, and real publication.
- Should concession categories draw from one shared total ticket pool, or each have a separate fixed allocation?
- Admission ticket issuance was excluded from the current demo; disclaimer rendering on issued tickets is a future ticket-output requirement unless scope is expanded.
- Digital confirmations and referral rewards require a purchase flow beyond the current listing-and-ticket-types demo. Delivery channel and external map behavior remain to be defined.
- How are third-party payment processing fees allocated?
- Define exact payout timing after event completion, later refund/dispute handling, rescheduling and the booked occurrence for repeating activities. Immediate pending-reward visibility is proposed, not yet approved.
- Which exact service does the owner mean by EPay?
- Select payment providers and verify supported methods, countries, currencies, settlement, refund handling and referral payouts before committing to payment coverage. Define launch coverage versus later integrations.
