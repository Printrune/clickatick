## Ship AI workshop kit

Role instructions, workflow, brief system, templates and the Next.js `starter/` come from the **Ship AI workshop kit** by Santos Hernandez / Ship AI (https://shipai.club), published for the Ship AI AZ GTM Hackathon, October 2026:
https://shipai.club/programs/zero-to-launch/hackathon/bots

Ship AI's original skills: `brief-to-site`, `about-page`, `seo-article`, `workshop-owner-language`. The kit also supplies `yc-interface-craft`, `intentional-ui-design` and `illustration-design-grammar`.

This folder is an adaptation for Claude Code. The original kit's README is kept at `docs/original-kit-README.md`, and the exact upstream skill revisions and file hashes are in `docs/sources.json` and `starter/skill-sources.json`.
