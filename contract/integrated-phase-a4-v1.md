# Integrated ticketing Phase A4 — public event and demo buyer contract v1

Date: 2026-10-21. Prospective contract from `brief/project.json` v98. Frontend review and a PM handoff are required **before code**. A4 is the first complete new Neon marketplace path. The full Lovable ticketing product remains the destination, while clickatick's conversation stays at `/creator`. A1 brand identity, A2 private drafts and A3 private media are prerequisites. Existing anonymous Blob demo listings and receipts remain separate at `/listing/[id]` and `/receipt/[id]` until this entire Neon flow is verified; this document does not delete or migrate them.

## Scope and truthful demo state

A brand owner may review and publish a complete brand draft, then cancel the resulting public event. Invited editors may prepare drafts after the staff contract, but publish/cancel remains owner-only. No post-publish edit exists in A4; the source draft becomes locked. A public visitor can view an event, choose an occurrence, ticket types and exact seats, obtain a server-calculated **demo quote**, and create a shareable **demo receipt**. There is no payment authorization, order, ticket issuance, seat hold, inventory decrement, admission credential, email, referral payout or actual sale. The receipt says **“Demo confirmation — no payment or reservation”** and contains no scannable QR/barcode. “Ticket price” is illustrative; `amountChargedMinor=0`. The configured 10% platform fee comes from the illustrative ticket subtotal, never adds a guest surcharge. Payment-method preferences and referral percentages remain configuration only.

The owner approved mixed numbered-seat and general-admission ticket types. Accessible seats are labelled and selectable by **any** demo visitor; no eligibility question is asked. The organizer chooses `per_occurrence` or `across_dates` capacity. In A4 both are configured limits only, because demo actions do not consume stock. UI and API must never call seats “available,” “held,” “sold” or “reserved.”

## Publication preconditions and canonical records

Migration 004 introduces `public_events(id UUID PK,organizer_id,source_draft_id UNIQUE,source_version,version,status published|cancelled,published_snapshot JSONB,published_at,cancelled_at,cancel_reason)`, normalized `public_occurrences`, `public_tiers`, `public_sections`, `public_seats`, `public_inventory_pools`, `public_media_refs`, `demo_quotes` and `demo_receipts`. IDs are immutable UUIDs; public URL `/events/{publicEventId}` never depends on brand slug or title. The source draft and public event belong to one brand. `public_media_refs` holds three optional slot references to ready private assets and extends A3 delete blocking even after cancellation; no public media is copied into Blob. The A3 asset deletion function checks **both** draft and public references. No old Supabase data, permissive RLS policy or Lovable client checkout is reused.

Before publish, validate the **current** A2 snapshot and `expectedVersion`, owner membership, title, description optional, type, timezone, at least one future occurrence, ticket format `digital|paper|both`, at least one named tier with integer USD price, capacity and required allocation, reviewed disclaimer and explicit refund policy. Validate business location address, exact lat/lng pair and ready photo; if `sameLocation=false`, validate separate event address/pin/photo. A cover image is optional, but if supplied must be ready and same-brand. Restrictions may be blank; the organizer reviews them as entered. A3 private upload and authenticated preview must be working before publish can pass required-photo checks. If A3 writes remain gated, return a field-specific 422 `PHOTO_NEEDED`; do not fabricate a photo or publish an incomplete event.

`inventory.dateScope` must be `per_occurrence|across_dates`; `tierScope` must be `shared|allocated`. `totalCapacity` is a positive integer. In allocated mode, each tier allocation is positive, all allocations are present, and their sum equals `totalCapacity`; in shared mode tier allocation fields are null. Configured seat geometry is bounded by A2 (50 sections, 10,000 seats); each section ID and row/number pair is unique. A seat's stable ID is generated at publish from its section/row/number and stored. `seatTierId=null` permits any `optional|required` tier; otherwise it permits that tier only. `seatPolicy:none` is general admission and never selects a seat. Mixed `none` and seated tiers are valid. A `required` or `optional` seat tier requires at least one compatible configured seat. Seat count is **not** required to equal total capacity because general admission may share the event; a single quote cannot select more seats than the compatible configured geometry. A future live-inventory contract must resolve real stock and seats separately.

Individual dates retain A2 local `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm` values. Weekly rules expand within the approved ≤366-day range, with at most 366 occurrences total. Publish converts each wall time to a UTC instant using the saved IANA timezone and rejects nonexistent or ambiguous local times with 422 `SCHEDULE_TIME_AMBIGUOUS` or `SCHEDULE_TIME_INVALID` at the exact date; it does not guess a DST offset. Occurrence IDs are stored and stable after publish; each record has `{id,startLocal,startsAtUtc,timeZone,durationMinutes,status:"scheduled"}`. No post-publish reschedule in A4. Past dates reject publication. Cross-midnight duration is allowed, but `endsAtUtc` is computed server-side. Public reads may include a past occurrence as historical information but quote requires a future scheduled occurrence.

Publication is one Neon transaction. Lock source draft and brand membership, validate snapshot/media and full occurrence/tier/seat expansion, claim idempotency `(owner subject,brand,draft,publish key)`, insert public records and references, then commit. `public_events.source_draft_id UNIQUE` is the durable publish lock: the A2 PATCH checks this table inside its draft-write transaction and returns 409 `PUBLISHED_LOCKED` once the row exists. Same key and same canonical request replays the same 201 event ID/URL; same key with different request returns 409. A second key for an already published source draft returns 409 `ALREADY_PUBLISHED` with the existing public ID, not another public event. An ownership or photo state race aborts without a partial event. The exact lock/read/write sequence must preserve A3 asset-delete exclusion.

## Private owner routes

All JSON uses `{data,error}` and `Cache-Control:no-store`. Field errors use `{path,code,message}`. Nonmember/foreign IDs return 404, disabled membership 403, and editor/viewer publish or cancel 403. The server derives Clerk subject; organizer ID in URL is never authority.

| Route | Request | Success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /api/v1/organizers/{org}/events/{draftId}/publish` | Same-origin JSON `{expectedVersion:integer,idempotencyKey:UUID}` ≤8 KiB | 201 `{event:{id,status:"published",version:1,sourceDraftId,publishedAt},publicUrl:"/events/{id}",warnings:[]}`. |
| `POST /api/v1/organizers/{org}/published-events/{eventId}/cancel` | Same-origin JSON `{expectedVersion:integer,idempotencyKey:UUID,reason:string}`; reason 0–500 chars | 200 `{event:{id,status:"cancelled",version,cancelledAt,reason},publicUrl}`. Idempotent replay returns the same body. |
| `GET /api/v1/organizers/{org}/published-events/{eventId}` | Owner/editor/viewer active member | 200 owner projection with source draft ID and status; no buyer PII exists. |

Cancellation is a transaction locking the public event, checking owner and version, claiming idempotency and changing status exactly once. A changed key on an already cancelled event returns 409 `ALREADY_CANCELLED`; same key/body replays 200. It does not delete the public detail, photo, receipt, or media references. It invalidates outstanding demo quotes and blocks new quote/checkout. Existing shareable demo receipts remain readable and show `eventStatus:"cancelled"`; they cannot imply a refund because nothing was charged. A4 has no automatic cancellation email or payout.

## Public catalog and event DTO

`GET /api/v1/events?cursor=&limit=` returns `200 {events:PublicEventSummary[],nextCursor}` with limit 1–50, default 20, stable `(publishedAt,id)` cursor, published noncancelled events only. Search/filter is later work. Summary: `{id,title,type,organizer:{id,name},location:{name,address,latitude,longitude},nextOccurrence:{id,startLocal,startsAtUtc,timeZone}|null,startingPriceMinor,currency:"USD",coverImageUrl,detailUrl:"/events/{id}",demo:true}`. No member email, Clerk ID, private Blob path, hidden draft or provider token leaks. `GET /api/v1/events/{id}` returns `200 {event:PublicEventDetail}` for published **or cancelled** events, else 404. Detail includes summary fields, description, restrictions, disclaimer, refundPolicy, ticketFormat, business and event location with map coordinates and photo URLs, cover URL, `status`, configured tiers, future/past occurrences, inventory date/tier scope and **configured** capacities, section geometry and accessible-seat counts, referral percentage as inactive configuration, and explicit `demo:true,seatSelectionReserves:false,paymentConnected:false`. It does not expose every configured seat in an unbounded response: total geometry is ≤10,000, and `GET /api/v1/events/{id}/occurrences/{occurrenceId}/seats?cursor=&limit=` pages selectable seat IDs, row/number, coordinates and accessible flag (limit 1–200) by `(sectionId,row,number)`.

The page `/events/{id}` consumes this new Neon API. Existing `/listing/{id}` continues to use the legacy Blob API. The marketplace `/` and `/discover` may show a clearly labelled Neon collection **only after** catalog → detail → quote → demo checkout → receipt passes browser/API/data checks. Until then, keep the current Blob collection operational and separate. No silent merge or URL redirect at schema creation.

Public image URLs are only `/api/v1/events/{eventId}/media/{slot}` with `slot=business|event-location|cover`. This server route reads a published/cancelled event's immutable `public_media_refs`, checks ready asset state, then streams from the private Blob store. A missing optional slot returns 404; a missing referenced Blob returns 503 and an operations alert, never a public Blob URL. Send allowlisted MIME, `Content-Disposition:inline`, `X-Content-Type-Options:nosniff`, same-origin resource policy and bounded cache headers. No direct signed Blob URL, browser Blob token, or arbitrary asset-ID proxy exists.

## Demo quote and receipt DTOs

`POST /api/v1/events/{eventId}/demo-quotes` accepts same-origin JSON ≤16 KiB:

```json
{"idempotencyKey":"uuid","occurrenceId":"uuid","items":[{"tierId":"uuid","quantity":2,"seatIds":["uuid","uuid"]}]}
```

At most 10 tier rows and 20 total tickets per quote; each tier appears once. `seatIds` are distinct across the whole request. For `seatPolicy:none`, the array is empty; for `required`, length equals quantity; for `optional`, length is at most quantity. Every chosen seat belongs to this event/section and is compatible with its tier. Accessible seats are selectable by any guest and are labelled in the response. A `per_occurrence` limit is checked against that occurrence's configured pool; an `across_dates` limit against the event-level configured pool. Allocated tier quantities and shared total are checked independently. Because no stock is decremented, this is a validation against **configured limits for one demo quote**, not a promise of availability against other demos. Event/occurrence must be published and future. No customer name, email, payment method, address or eligibility proof is accepted.

Success `201 {quote:{id,eventId,occurrenceId,items:[{tierId,name,quantity,unitPriceMinor,seatIds,seatLabels}],ticketSubtotalMinor,platformFeeMinor,organizerShareMinor,amountChargedMinor:0,currency:"USD",expiresAt,demo:true,seatSelectionReserves:false},checkoutUrl:"/events/{eventId}/demo-checkout?quote={quoteId}"}`. Fee is 10% of subtotal rounded half-up to integer cents; organizer share is subtotal minus fee, before any inactive referral illustration. No guest surcharge or processing fee is added. `quoteId` is random and opaque. Quotes expire after 15 minutes, are bound to published event version and hold no seats. Same idempotency key+same canonical selection replays the same quote; changed selection with same key returns 409. A cancelled event or stale event version returns 409. No quote is called a booking.

`POST /api/v1/events/{eventId}/demo-checkouts` accepts same-origin JSON `{quoteId:UUID,idempotencyKey:UUID}` ≤8 KiB. In a single transaction, lock/read quote and event, require nonexpired quote, matching event/version and published future occurrence, revalidate server-priced items and configured seat compatibility, then persist one immutable `demo_receipt` for this quote and idempotency key. No inventory pool, seat status, payment or order table is written. The checkout has no form fields for buyer identity. Success `201 {receipt:{id,eventId,eventStatus:"published",occurrenceId,items:[{tierId,name,quantity,seatIds,seatLabels}],ticketSubtotalMinor,platformFeeMinor,organizerShareMinor,amountChargedMinor:0,currency:"USD",demo:true,admissionValid:false,createdAt},receiptUrl:"/demo/receipts/{id}"}`. Same key/body replays the same receipt; a second key for the same quote returns 409 `QUOTE_ALREADY_USED` with the existing receipt ID. No email send or printable admission ticket is claimed.

`GET /api/v1/demo/receipts/{receiptId}` returns the receipt's public no-PII projection with current event status. `/demo/receipts/{id}` is its shareable page. Receipt IDs are random unguessable UUIDs and are not indexed or listed. The page repeats the demo/no-payment/no-reservation warning and has no barcode. A cancelled event remains visible with a cancellation notice. Receipt reads use `Cache-Control:no-store` so status updates show promptly.

## Security, errors and release tests

All public IDs and cursors are strictly parsed. All reads have explicit small projections. Public detail and seats are bounded; never return Blob paths, all 10,000 seat rows at once, Clerk IDs or device draft data. Mutating routes enforce origin, JSON type and size, per-route idempotency and Neon uniqueness. Anonymous quote/checkout uses an HttpOnly SameSite anonymous demo cookie minted by the server solely for abuse control; store only its hash, not IP or PII in receipts. Apply both a per-cookie and global per-event quota, returning 429 with `Retry-After`; if the cookie is unavailable, apply the stricter event quota. These demo quotas need exact numbers agreed before implementation. No optimistic client price, referral reward or capacity claim is trusted. Expected errors: 400 invalid body, 401 owner sign-in, 403 role/origin, 404 nonpublic/foreign ID, 409 stale/cancelled/replayed-key conflict, 413 body too large, 422 incomplete draft or invalid selected seat, 429 rate limit and 503 missing Clerk/Neon/Blob.

Backend owns migration 004, publish/cancel transaction, recurrence/timezone resolver, read projections, public media proxy, quote/receipt calculation and idempotency. Frontend owns owner review/publish/cancel UX, public event/detail/seat picker, truthful quote and demo checkout, and shareable receipt. Both must agree DTOs and status/error copy before code. Meaningful tests: owner/editor/foreign-brand permissions, incomplete venue/photo/pin, DST gap/overlap, mixed GA/seated quote, accessible-seat choice, duplicate/foreign seat rejection, date-scope validation, concurrent publish/cancel/idempotency, quote/cancel race, zero-charge checkout, no stock decrement, no PII, media route boundary, legacy Blob isolation, responsive and keyboard buyer flow. A connected-service browser/API/data test gates marketplace cutover and any live success claim.

PM decisions still needed before implementation: the exact anonymous demo abuse limits, and how long no-PII demo receipts should remain shareable. Published-event edits remain unapproved and absent from A4. A3 private-media quotas remain pending; required-photo publication cannot go live until that gate is resolved. No app code, database or service is changed by this prospective contract.
